Happy to announce that DreamAI has 12 papers accepted at EMNLP 2026 conference (6 main conference, 6 findings). See you at EMNLP!



EMNLP 2026 Main Conference

\[1] Nguyen Phu An, Nguyễn Dũng, An Lưu, mentor Trung Le, collaborating mentor Thien Huu Nguyen, "HyperProve: Answer-Guided Hypergraph Expansion for Multi-Hop Question Answering"



\[2] Hải An, Phung Huy, Trương Minh Phúc, collaborating mentor Thanh Hong Nguyen, mentor Thanh-Toan Do và mentor Trung Le, "SUGAR: Spectral and Geometry-Aware Alignment for Matryoshka Representation Distillation"



\[3] The Anh Tran, Nguyễn Xuan Thành, mentor Nguyễn Anh, collaborating mentor Đinh Viết Sang, Thien Huu Nguyen, "PGA-KD: Principal Geometry Alignment and Semantic Consistency for VLM2Vec Distillation"



\[4] Tung Nguyen Son, Dat Phan Trong, mentor Nguyễn Anh, Hoang Thai Dinh, Diep Nguyen, "LLM Feedback for Topic Model with Preference Optimization and Contrastive Learning"

EMNLP 2026 Findings



\[5] Hoàng Thành Đạt, Phong Đào, Hải Nam, Franck Dernoncourt, mentor Trung Le, collaborating mentor Thien Huu Nguyen, "EventKD: Event-Aware Knowledge Distillation for Generative Event Extraction"



\[6] Tung Nguyen Son, Hưng Trần, Hoang Thai Dinh, Diep Nguyen, mentors Trung Le, Nguyễn Anh "Complementary Lattice Correction for Post-Training Quantization"

\[7] Trần Hoàng, Tiến Đạt, Phạm Khánh Chi, Trương Minh Phúc, Quyen Tran, collaborating mentor Thien Huu Nguyen, mentors Thanh-Toan Do, Trung Le, "A Survey on Knowledge Distillation of Vision-Language Models"

\[8] Nguyễn Tiến Phát, Minh Chu Xuan, Phung Huy, mentors Nguyễn Anh, Trung Le, collaborating mentor Thien Huu Nguyen, "Don't Generate, Just Score: Enhancing Topic Coherence with Masked Semantic Alignment"

\[9] Minh Khoi Nguyen Do, Hồng Đăng, Danh Vinh, Nguyễn Quốc Nhật Minh, mentors Nguyễn Anh, Trung Le, "M-VLM2Vec: Structure-Preserving Matryoshka Representation Learning for Vision-Language Embedding"

\[10] Huy Huu Nguyen, Chien Van Nguyen, Franck Dernoncourt, Ryan A. Rossi, Linh Ngo Van, Jieyang Chen, Thien Huu Nguyen, "CRISP: Cliff-aware Residual-free Input-adaptive Sparse Prefilling with Entropy-Motivated Routing" (main)
